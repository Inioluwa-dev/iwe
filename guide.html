How to Use Book Tracker

Plain, step-by-step lessons that explain every page and every button. No tech knowledge needed.

Built by Olayoriju Inioluwa · 09133770970

1 Start Here: Your First 5 Minutes

This app helps you sell books to your coursemates and collect class dues without paper lists. Everything is stored on your own phone or computer. No account. No internet needed.

The 4 pages

There are only 4 pages. Each one has exactly one job:

  • Home — see all your coursemates and tap to record who has paid.
  • Add & Books — add new people and new books.
  • Money — see your costs, sales, and profit. It fills itself in.
  • Dues — collect class dues that are not for books.

How to move around

  • On a phone: tap the 4 icons in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  • On a computer: click the 4 items in the menu on the left side.

Do these 3 things first

  1. Go to Add & Books and add your coursemates' names.
  2. Still on Add & Books, add the books everyone must buy, with their prices.
  3. Go to Home. As people pay you, tap the round button on their book.

Read the next pages one by one. Each page of this guide explains one page of the app.

2 Home: Your Daily Dashboard

Home is where you work every day. It shows every coursemate and the books each person must buy. When somebody pays you, you record it here.

The numbers at the top

The two dark boxes show Coursemates (how many people you have added) and Books (how many different book titles exist). They update by themselves.

Finding one person

  • Search box: start typing a name or matric number. The list gets shorter as you type. Clear the box to see everyone again.
  • Filter buttons: All shows everything. Pending shows only books people have not paid for — use this to see who still owes you. Paid and Completed show only those.

Recording payment: tap the round button

Every book has a small round button on it. Each tap moves the book one step forward:

○ Pending → ₦ Paid → ✓ Completed → (back to Pending)

  • ○ Pending (yellow) — the person has not paid. Every new book starts here.
  • ₦ Paid (blue) — the person paid you, but you have not given them the book yet.
  • ✓ Completed (green) — the person paid and received the book. Nothing left to do.

If you tap too many times, just keep tapping. It goes round in a circle and comes back.

The buttons on each person

  • + (plus): give this one person one extra book. Other people are not affected.
  • ✎ (pencil): change the person's name or matric number, or remove them from class dues.
  • × on a book: removes that one book from that one person only. The app will ask you to confirm first.
  • × on the person: deletes the person and all their books. The app will ask you to confirm first.

Under each name you will see progress like 2/5 completed. It means 2 out of 5 books are fully completed.

Export and danger zone

  • Export CSV downloads your whole list as a spreadsheet file. This is also how you make backups.
  • Clear All Data sits far below in the red Danger zone. It deletes everything and cannot be undone. Always export first.

If the list is long, use Previous and Next at the bottom to move between pages. The search box searches all pages at once.

3 Adding Coursemates

Go to Add & Books, then use the Add Coursemates box. You can add one person or 100 people at once.

How to type the names

Put each person on their own line. Write the name alone, or the name, a comma, then the matric number:

Adaeze Okafor, EKSU001
John Doe, EKSU002
Jane Smith
  • The comma is what separates the name from the matric number. Do not put a comma inside a name.
  • Matric number is optional. A person with only a name is fine.
  • If a name already exists, the app skips it instead of adding it twice.

The two checkboxes (read before you tap Add)

  • "Assign all currently shared books": leave this ON if you already added books. Every new person will automatically receive all the books, marked Pending. Turn it OFF only if these people should start with zero books.
  • "Exclude from Class Dues": turn this ON only if these people must never appear on the Dues page (for example, executives who do not pay dues). Everyone else should leave it OFF.

Tap Add Coursemates. The box clears, and the new names appear on Home immediately.

4 Importing from Excel or CSV

Use this only if you already have a long list in Excel. If you are starting fresh, typing names directly (page 3) is easier.

The golden rule

You cannot upload just any Excel file. The file must use the app's own columns. The safe method is: Export first, edit that file, then Import it back.

Do it step by step

  1. Go to Home and tap Export CSV. A spreadsheet file downloads.
  2. Open that file in Excel, WPS, or Google Sheets.
  3. Keep the top header row exactly as it is: Name, Matric No, Book, Status. Never rename or delete these headers.
  4. Add one row per person. For the Status column, write only pending, paid, or completed (small letters).
  5. To give one person extra books, add more rows with the same name. You may leave the name empty on the extra rows.
  6. Save the file, keeping the .csv or .xlsx format.
  7. Go to Add & Books, choose the file under Import Coursemates, and tap Import.

The app tells you how many people and books were imported. Names that already exist are not added twice. But be careful: book rows are added again on every import, so import each file only once.

5 Shared Books and Prices

A shared book is one textbook that many coursemates must buy — for example, "Introduction to Accounting". You create shared books under Add & Books.

Buying price vs selling price

  • Buying price is what the book costs you — what you pay the lecturer or supplier.
  • Selling price is what each student pays you.

Example: you buy each copy at ₦1,500 and students pay you ₦2,000. Your profit is ₦500 on every copy. The Money page does this maths for you.

If you leave both prices at 0, the Money page will show ₦0 profit. Always type the real prices when you add a book.

What "Add & Assign Book to Everyone" does

One tap gives the new book to every person currently on your list, each marked Pending. People you add later also receive it automatically, as long as the "Assign all currently shared books" box is ON when you add them.

The Shared Books List

Below the form, the list shows every shared book with two counts: Paid (how many people paid but have no book yet) and Completed (how many finished everything).

  • ✎ (pencil): change the book title or its prices. New prices apply to the Money maths immediately.
  • × (cross): removes this book from everyone at once. The app will ask you to confirm first.

Need to give a book to only one person instead of everyone? Go to Home and tap the + button on that person's row.

6 Money and Profit

The Money page is fully automatic. You never type anything here. It reads your Home records and calculates profit for you.

Only paid books count

Only books marked Paid or Completed enter the maths. Books still Pending are ignored, because no money has moved yet.

The three big numbers

  • Total Cost — what you spent. Buying price × number of paid books.
  • Total Revenue — what students paid you. Selling price × number of paid books.
  • Total Profit — Revenue minus Cost. The box turns green when you are gaining and red when you are losing.

Worked example: 10 students paid for a book you buy at ₦1,500 and sell at ₦2,000. Cost = 10 × ₦1,500 = ₦15,000. Revenue = 10 × ₦2,000 = ₦20,000. Profit = ₦5,000.

The table below

Each row is one book, so you can see which book makes the most profit. The last Total row adds everything up.

Clear Financials deletes all books and all money records. Your people list stays, but every book assignment is gone. Export a backup before you touch it.

7 Class Dues

Use the Dues page for money that is NOT for books — for example, department or association dues where everybody pays the same fixed amount.

Step 1: set the amount

Type one fixed amount at the top (for example ₦5,000) and tap Save Amount. Every person on the list now owes exactly that amount.

The two dark boxes update at once: Total Dues Expected (amount × number of people — 40 people × ₦5,000 = ₦200,000) and Total Dues Collected (amount × number of people you have ticked).

Step 2: tick people as they pay

Each row has a round button. Tap ✓ when a person pays — their row turns blue and says Paid. Tapped by mistake? Tap the ✗ to change them back to Pending. Nothing is permanent.

  • Search box and filters work exactly like on Home. Use the Pending filter to see who still owes.
  • ✖ (second button): removes this person from the Dues page only. They stay on Home with all their books.
  • Export Dues CSV downloads the dues list as a spreadsheet. Keep it as your receipt record.

People who must not pay dues

Anyone ticked "Exclude from Class Dues" is hidden from this page and is NOT counted in Total Expected. To bring such a person back: go to Home, tap ✎ on their row, uncheck the box, and save.

8 Backing Up Your Data

Your records live only inside this browser on this device. That keeps things private and fast, but it also means your records disappear if you clear your browser history, use incognito mode, reset your phone, or move to a new phone. Backups protect you.

Step 1: download your backups

  1. Go to Add & Books and scroll to "Your Data & Backups".
  2. Tap Download Books Backup (CSV) and Download Dues Backup (CSV).
  3. Keep the two files somewhere safe — your phone storage, Google Drive, or send them to yourself on WhatsApp.

Step 2: restore when you need to

  1. Open the app in your (new) browser.
  2. Go to Add & Books and upload your saved Books CSV with the Import tool.
  3. Your people, books, and book statuses come back.

Know what a backup does NOT bring back: book prices, the dues amount, and dues paid ticks. After restoring, type the prices again, save the dues amount again, and re-tick who paid using your Dues CSV as the record.

Make it a habit: download fresh backups at the end of every week you collect money.

9 Fixing Common Problems

Quick answers to the problems people meet most:

  • I cannot find a name. Clear the search box, tap the All filter, and check other pages with Previous and Next. The person may be on another page.
  • I added the same person twice. Open one of the rows and tap ✎ to confirm which one is correct, then tap × on the wrong one to delete it.
  • I tapped the status button too far. Keep tapping. It moves Pending → Paid → Completed → Pending in a circle.
  • Money shows ₦0. Two usual causes: the book prices are still 0 (fix them with ✎ in the Shared Books List), or nobody is marked Paid yet.
  • A person is missing from Dues. They were excluded. Go to Home, tap ✎ on their row, uncheck "Exclude from Class Dues", and save.
  • I deleted something by mistake. Delete cannot be undone. Import your latest backup CSV to bring the records back, then re-type prices and re-tick dues.
  • I changed phones and everything is gone. The records lived in your old browser. If you have a backup CSV, import it. If not, you must add everything afresh — this is why weekly backups matter.
