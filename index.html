Add People & Books

Step 1: add your coursemates below. Step 2: add the books everyone needs. New people automatically get all shared books.

Add Coursemates

Type one or more names (with optional matric numbers separated by a comma). Put each person on a new line.

Import Coursemates

Have a long list in Excel? Export a CSV from Home first, add your names in the same format, then upload it here.

CSV format: Name, Matric No, Book, Status. Extra book rows can leave Name and Matric No blank.

Shared Books & Prices

A shared book is one book many coursemates must buy. Set what it costs you and what you charge.

Add New Shared Book

Adding a shared book will assign it to all current coursemates.

Your Data & Backups

Everything is stored privately on this device and browser — no account, no internet needed. But if you clear your browser data, use incognito mode, or switch phones, your records will be lost. Protect your work:

• Download backups regularly with the buttons below (you can also export from Home and Dues).
• Restore anytime by uploading a saved CSV file with the Import tool above.
